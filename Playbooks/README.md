# Playbooks

Playbooks are on-demand guidance for recurring kinds of work.

A playbook explains the purpose, inputs, desired output, useful construction
guidance, and important failure modes for one kind of work. It states the
outcome and the properties that matter, then trusts the agent to choose an
effective path. It does not prescribe an agent's internal reasoning.

Which playbook to read, and the condition that should trigger it, is listed in
[`Project Details/Project Details.md`](../Project%20Details/Project%20Details.md#research-playbooks),
alongside the project-wide research standards, stages, change rules, and
completion conditions that playbooks do not restate.
